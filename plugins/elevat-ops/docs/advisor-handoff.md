# Handing the finances to a licensed advisor

Models here prepare and inform. A licensed professional decides. This page is the packet to give them.

## Who to hire
- **Fee-only fiduciary financial planner** (paid by you, not by commissions). Look for CFP® holders; search CFP Board "Let's Make a Plan", NAPFA or the Garrett Planning Network (hourly planners).
- **CPA or enrolled agent** for the LLC, quarterly estimates, reserve percentage, owner pay and the Solo 401(k) (ELE-32, ELE-36, ELE-41).
- **Attorney** for LLC standing, trust and contracts (ELE-29, ELE-31, ELE-33).

Check each person before sharing data: SEC Investment Adviser Public Disclosure (adviserinfo.sec.gov), FINRA BrokerCheck, your state CPA board. Ask in writing: fiduciary at all times? how are you paid? any products you sell?

## Questions to bring
1. How should personal trading relate to my goals at all? Is paper trading enough, or should I skip it?
2. Emergency fund, debt, and order of priorities before any investing.
3. Tax reserve percentage of LLC deposits; quarterly estimate dates.
4. Owner pay: draw now, payroll or S-corp when profit justifies it.
5. Solo 401(k) and other tax-advantaged accounts once the LLC is profitable.
6. Which holdings (if any) have long, documented, low-cost track records suited to my timeline and risk.

## Packet (prepared by agents, read-only, from connected sources)
- Account balances and 12-month cash flow (PocketSmith, QuickBooks).
- LLC profit and loss, receivables, revenue to date (QuickBooks, Stripe).
- Open items and deadlines (Linear P-ELE-5).
- Paper-trading results, labeled paper and untested with real money.

Share only with the people you hired, over a channel they provide. Never paste account numbers or keys into chat.

## Rules for models while an advisor is involved
- Do not contradict or front-run the advisor; list differences as questions for them.
- No live trades, transfers or account changes by agents. Ever.
